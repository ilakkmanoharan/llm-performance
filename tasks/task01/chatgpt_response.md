# Is Consciousness a Quantum Particle? — Tweet Thread

**Tweet 1:**  
Is consciousness a *quantum particle* shaping our perception of the world? Short answer: No. There’s **no evidence** that consciousness is a fundamental particle like an electron or photon.

**Tweet 2:**  
Neuroscience sees consciousness as an **emergent process**—a coordinated pattern of electrical and chemical activity across massive neural networks.

**Tweet 3:**  
Some researchers (Penrose, Hameroff) suggest that *quantum effects* inside neurons might influence consciousness. Fascinating idea, but still **highly speculative** and unproven.

**Tweet 4:**  
Even these theories don’t claim consciousness *is* a quantum particle—they propose quantum **processes** might contribute to the experience of consciousness.

**Tweet 5:**  
Most physicists argue the brain is too warm, wet, and noisy for delicate quantum states to last long enough to affect cognition in a meaningful way.

**Tweet 6:**  
So the best current scientific understanding: consciousness isn’t a particle—it’s a **process** emerging from complex neural computation.

**Tweet 7:**  
Still, the question lives at the edge of physics, neuroscience, and philosophy: *How does subjective experience arise from matter?* The mystery is far from solved.
